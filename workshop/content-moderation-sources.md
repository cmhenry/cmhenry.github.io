It is short on purpose. A canonical reference doc is a personal canon, not an exhaustive list.

## Finding new IDs

To add a journal or author:

1. Go to [openalex.org](https://openalex.org) and search the name.
2. Open the result page.
3. The ID is in the URL — it's the part after the last `/`, starting with `S` (journals) or `A` (authors).
4. Paste it here with a one-line note on why it's in your canon.
