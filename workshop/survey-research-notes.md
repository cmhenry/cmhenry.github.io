# Survey research — presenter notes

Target: 25 minutes: wording 8, coding 8, simulation 6, project application 3. Slides and `survey-practical.md` adapt the current workshop draft's survey tasks. The website keeps its existing presentation design; the private source repository remains unchanged.

## Preparation

Open the complete practical. All prompts include the input, task, and output expectations. All responses are constructed teaching examples; no participant data or confidential transcripts are needed. Use a prepared model interface if available, otherwise work manually. This content revision is not a live model rehearsal.

## Slides 1–4: wording

Participants assess the two items before model output. Keep the intended construct fixed. The broader second question is not an automatic improvement: it changes the target. Discuss response scales and what “de-identified” and “commercial service” leave unspecified. Removing names does not necessarily anonymize a transcript.

The candidate revision in the practical is not an answer key or a validated instrument. Ask for cognitive-interview probes with human participants. Do not present a model's simulation of a respondent as equivalent evidence.

## Slides 5–7: coding

Apply the supplied codebook to all three constructed responses. Keep human judgments before the model comparison. A is plausibly P, B is V, and C is E plus V under these rules. Discuss any disagreement rather than claiming validated gold labels.

Distinguish changing the construct/codebook from improving a classifier. If definitions change, preserve both versions and recode all cases. Three examples support discussion, not a performance estimate. Explain held-out evaluation and error effects on the intended research conclusion without starting a data-collection exercise.

## Slides 8–9: synthetic respondents

The important distinction is the inferential purpose. Generated cases can test skip logic while supplying no evidence about a population's opinions. Matching observed marginals does not establish joint distributions or subgroup validity. Do not claim that every possible use of synthetic respondents is invalid; require evidence appropriate to the particular use.

## Slide 10: transfer

Allow three minutes for one proposed use, a comparison, and a boundary. Rejecting a use can be a well-supported outcome. If the session runs late, shorten the model demonstration rather than the human assessment or debrief.
