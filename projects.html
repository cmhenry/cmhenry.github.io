VIRENA

Indirect Effects of Content Moderation Errors: A Chatroom Experiment with AI Agents. VIRENA is an experimental platform simulating WhatsApp-style social media chatrooms. Prolific participants interact in real-time with LLM-powered bot personas under randomized moderation conditions — over-moderation, under-moderation, and regular moderation. The experiment tests how moderation errors shape conversation dynamics and perceptions of online spaces.

With Karsten Donnay, Giuliano Formisano, Emma Hoes, Jonathan Klues, Adiba Mahbub Proma, Fabrizio Gilardi (PI).

Online War Exposure

Principal investigator

A collaboration with Jessica Feezell at the University of New Mexico on how repeated, vivid, first-person audiovisual exposure to war shapes political socialization. We are developing survey experiments and data-donation studies to examine how selected conflict examples shape representative inferences, attention, and political judgment. The project is supported by an SNSF Scientific Exchange grant and a separate University of Zürich Institute of Political Science research grant.

With Jessica Feezell (University of New Mexico).

Dissertation

"Governance, Competition, & Extremism: How the Structure of Social Media Platforms Radicalizes Communities." Platform governance structures create competitive dynamics between communities that systematically drive radicalization. A comparative study of digital architectures and how they shape collective behavior.

With Jennifer Larson (co-chair), Cassy Dorff (co-chair), Emily Ritter, Anita Gohdes (Hertie School).
